English Considered Harmful

How Morphological Poverty Pollutes Language Model Training

Adam Zachary Wasserman, Open Honest Foundation. Preprint, 6 April 2026.

Download the PDF

Abstract

English is the dominant language in language model training. This paper presents experimental evidence that it actively interferes with learning structurally explicit patterns.

Three controlled comparisons: French against English, interleaved French and English against French alone, and Rust against Rust mixed with English. In each case, mixing English with a structurally richer language degrades both perplexity and structural pattern acquisition.

At 125M parameters the effect is large enough to matter for anyone assembling a multilingual corpus, and it points the opposite way from the usual assumption that more data helps.
