Process Discipline as the Key Variable in AI-Assisted Enterprise Software Development

A Natural Experiment

Adam Zachary Wasserman, Open Honest Foundation. Preprint, 31 March 2026.

Download the PDF

Abstract

The same two programmers, using the same AI tools, built two applications under two process conditions. The difference was not the team and not the tooling. It was who held authority over the development lifecycle.

Under a structured, AI-specific SDLC the platform satisfied 18 of 18 enterprise dimensions derived from SOC 2, NIST and OWASP, at eight to ten times elite productivity benchmarks. When process authority shifted to non-technical stakeholders, the second application satisfied 2 of 18, with no tests, no quality pipelines and no code review.

Measured across 287 FTE-days, 1.48 million lines added and 811,000 lines removed. The exogenous change in process authority is what makes this a natural experiment rather than a comparison of teams.
